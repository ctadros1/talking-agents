# Scientific-track presentation questions


- **What is new?** A task-specific cue-information objective and a controlled
  implementation/evaluation in this split-information task. Positive
  signalling, PPO and emergent communication are prior work. The direct
  baseline is unmodified recurrent IPPO; no superiority to the full Eccles
  method is claimed.
- **Is that comparison pre-registered?** The channel benchmark and v2 claims
  were frozen prospectively. The full unbiased-method ablation is a separate
  exploratory comparison added after seeing their outcomes, as disclosed.
- **Why can a positive confidence interval still fail v2?** The frozen rule
  also requires a minimum effect size. Claim B fails that rule, and Claim C
  is not reached in the fixed sequence.
- **Why not just show mutual information?** Unheard scouts can be informative
  under the same bias. MI describes signalling; the intervention tests
  dependence on message identities.
- **Why is the unseen split not a compositionality proof?** Its restricted
  target support provides shortcuts. The fixed reference and structural
  metrics do not establish transfer of both properties.
- **How does this use the course?** Topic 7 covers agent reinforcement
  learning and multi-agent systems. Local perception, recurrent memory,
  separate policies and shared rewards are this project's concrete implementation. These small learned
  controllers isolate those mechanisms without a pretrained language model.

- **What is the independent unit?** One separately trained seed. The 200 fixed scenarios per split are reused across models. Seed-bootstrap intervals describe training variability conditional on that set, not uncertainty across task generators or matching designs.
- **Does the failure audit explain composition?** It partitions existing outcomes into no pickup, wrong pickup, correct pickup without delivery and success. Only correct pickup without delivery directly identifies a post-pick navigation/time failure. The oracle also declines, so the total unseen gap is not a pure measure of language composition.
- **Does passing the seen rule prove a fifteen-point minimum gain?** No. The registered conjunction is a point estimate at least fifteen points and an interval excluding zero. It is not a ninety-five-percent lower bound of fifteen points.
- **What study would strengthen originality?** Match the original trajectory-based signalling method at the same budget, and separately redesign the combination split to remove support shortcuts. Those are future experiments, not completed results.
